Privacy Policy

Movement Tracker · last updated November 15, 2026

1. Summary

Movement Tracker (“the app”) is published by TeamCoolApps. It records where you go while you choose to record — the places you stop at and the routes between them — and shows them as a timeline, a schematic network map and statistics.

  • The app collects your precise location, but only while a recording you started is running. You start it with a tap on Start line and end it with End line; a notification is shown the whole time.
  • The app does not use background location and never starts recording on its own (not at boot, not on a schedule, not from a geofence).
  • Your location history is stored only on your phone and is never uploaded. We (the developer) never receive any data about you.
  • The app goes online for one optional feature only: the street map, which is off until you turn it on and downloads map images from a map service (see section 5).
  • It tracks only the phone’s own user. There is no live sharing, no remote viewing and no hidden mode.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting.

2. Data we collect

The developer collects nothing. We have no server, so no location, personal data, usage data or analytics ever reach us.

The only third party the app ever contacts is the map service described in section 5, and only while you use the optional street map. It receives the map area being shown, not your history.

The location data described in section 3 is collected by the app for you, kept on your device, and leaves it only in files you choose to export or back up yourself.

Everything is stored in the app’s private storage. The app opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so this data is not copied to your Google account.

5. Network activity and third parties

The app makes no network requests unless you turn on the optional street map (Settings → Privacy → Street map, or Show streets on a map). It is off by default, and the app explains what is sent before you turn it on. Recording never uses the network. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social or cloud SDK. The schematic network map is drawn on your phone from your own stops.

Street map. While a street map is on screen, the app (using the open-source MapLibre library) downloads map images over an encrypted connection from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org), a map service run by a third party. Each request names the part of the map being shown, so OpenFreeMap can infer roughly which area you are looking at, together with your IP address and technical request details such as the app and library version. Your recorded history, places, stops, notes and settings are never sent. OpenFreeMap handles these requests under its own terms. Downloaded map images are kept in a cache in the app’s private storage and are deleted when you turn the street map off or delete all your data. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Open in maps app. On a stop you can tap Open in maps app; that stop’s position (and its name, if you gave it one) is then handed to the maps app you choose, which handles it under its own privacy policy.

Positions come from your phone’s location system. Where Google Play services is installed, the app uses its fused location provider (GPS, Wi-Fi, mobile network and sensors); otherwise it uses Android’s built-in location providers. When Google Location Accuracy is turned on in your phone’s settings, Google may collect location data to improve location accuracy under Google’s Privacy Policy; this is a device setting, independent of this app, which never sends your data to Google or anyone else.

When you share an export or backup through the system share sheet, the app hands the file to the app you choose; the developer does not see what you share.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the developer collects no personal information from anyone.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your history stays on your phone until you delete it. You can:

  • delete part of a day’s line or a saved place in the app, or a date range in Settings → Your data;
  • set Settings → Your data → Keep history so history older than the period you choose is deleted automatically;
  • tap Settings → Your data → Delete all my data to erase every recording, place and edit at once (this also clears downloaded map images);
  • turn off Settings → Privacy → Street map to stop all network use and clear downloaded map images;
  • clear the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → Movement Tracker → Storage, or uninstall the app.

Exports and backups you saved are your own files and remain until you delete them. The developer holds no copy of anything.

8. Security

App data is held in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s built-in application sandboxing, and is excluded from Android cloud backup. An optional app lock asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock and hides the app’s content from screenshots and the recent-apps view. The app is distributed through the Google Play Store, which verifies the developer signing key on every update.
